# ADR 000 — ADR Template

> Use this template for new Architecture Decision Records. Copy it to
> `NNN-short-title.md`, incrementing the number.

## Title

Short noun-phrase title.

## Status

Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNN] | Deprecated

## Date

YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What is the issue or force that motivates this decision? State the problem,
the constraints, and the alternatives considered. Be factual.

## Decision

What is the change or choice being made. State it clearly and imperatively.

## Consequences

What follows from this decision — positive, negative, and neutral. Note any
new risks, required follow-up work, or constraints imposed on future ADRs.

## Alternatives considered

List the realistic alternatives and why they were rejected. Keep this brief
but specific enough to prevent re-litigating.

## Compliance

How compliance with this decision is verified (lint rules, tests, review
checklist, etc.).
